# Team AFM_Ericsson

**AFM_Ericsson** is building [`autofm`](https://github.com/telvarix/autofm) - an
autonomous fault management system that predicts faults in Open RAN (O-RAN)
networks before they cause outages.

We are three telecom engineers who work at Ericsson. We bring hands-on experience in O-RAN, network management,
software engineering, and system architecture - the domain knowledge behind
`autofm`'s multi-level telemetry approach. Our goal at the European AI Hackathon
2026 is to GPU-accelerate and scale the fault-prediction pipeline.
